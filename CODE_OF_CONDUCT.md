# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and resource authors with respect. Welcome different backgrounds,
languages, experience levels, and good-faith disagreement. Critique work with specific
evidence and offer corrections constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized comments, doxxing, impersonation,
spam, and deliberate license or author misrepresentation are not welcome.
Do not publish private messages, credentials, or personal information without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close discussions, warn
participants, or restrict participation. They will consider context, explain actions
where appropriate, and apply these standards consistently.

For a routine moderation concern, contact a repository maintainer through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. If a maintainer
is involved, contact another maintainer or use GitHub's abuse reporting tools.

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and community spaces
where someone represents this project. It is original project policy under MIT.
